## Data and history

Code and measurement scripts were developed with AI participation;
the author designed, restored and checked the hardware. Documents
distinguish his statements, simulations and real measurements.
An open licence does not confirm a 200 m range, accuracy or IP68 protection.

The root manifest describes the current publication folder. The
measurement-series manifest remains unchanged and also contains hashes
of primary captures retained in the author's working archive. The new
licence does not change numerical results or file provenance.
