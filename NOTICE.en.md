## Data and history

Code and measurement procedures were developed with AI assistance; the author designed, restored and tested the hardware. Documents distinguish his statements, simulations and real measurements. An open licence does not confirm a 200 m range, accuracy or IP68 protection.

The root manifest describes the publication package. The measurement-series manifest is unchanged and also contains hashes of primary captures retained in the author's working archive. The licence does not change numerical results or file provenance.
